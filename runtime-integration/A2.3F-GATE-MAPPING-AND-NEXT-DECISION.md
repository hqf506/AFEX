## Fail-closed review sequence

1. Normalize every response using the A2.3E schema.
2. Reject unsafe evidence and mark missing facts explicitly.
3. Resolve provider contradictions before testing.
4. Do not begin runtime tests until G1, G2, and G6 prerequisites permit a safe isolated test.
5. Require runtime evidence for G3, G4, G5, G10, and G11.
6. Require capacity, secret-lifecycle, and observability approval for G8, G9, and G12.
7. Record each gate as pass, fail, or unknown. Unknown is not approval.
8. Have designated reviewers select and sign one outcome; no artifact selects it automatically.

## Permitted next decision

- `TRANSACTION_POOL_ARCHITECTURE_APPROVED` when all transaction-pool gates pass.
- `TRANSACTION_POOL_ARCHITECTURE_BLOCKED` when a required transaction-pool property fails but disposition still requires review.
- `SEPARATE_COMMAND_SERVICE_REQUIRED` when the approved trusted topology cannot be safely hosted through the evaluated Vercel/pool path.
- `INSUFFICIENT_EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE` when any required gate remains unknown.
- `CONFLICTING_PROVIDER_EVIDENCE` when authoritative evidence conflicts and cannot yet be reconciled.

No outcome is selected in A2.3F.
